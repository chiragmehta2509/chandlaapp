# REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

Effective Date: 19 Jun 2026

## 1. Digital Services

Chandla Book provides digital services and subscription-based features.

These may include:

* Event Packs
* Premium Features
* Invitation Services
* Guest Pay Features
* QR Features
* Analytics Features

## 2. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be considered only if:

* Duplicate payment occurred
* Payment was deducted but service was not activated
* Technical failure prevented delivery

Proof may be required.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are generally non-refundable:

* Activated Digital Services
* Consumed Credits
* Generated Invitations
* Generated Videos
* Downloaded Digital Content
* Used Premium Features

## 4. Cancellation

Future purchases may be cancelled before activation.

Activated services cannot be cancelled.

## 5. Duplicate Payments

Verified duplicate payments will be refunded to the original payment method.

## 6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days.

Actual credit timelines depend on banks and payment providers.
